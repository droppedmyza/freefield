Make an effect

Say what you become, then what happens. Add "with a before/after reveal", "side by side with my photo" or titled "YOUR TITLE" if you want them. The builder assembles a ComfyUI graph from tested stages, checks it, renders a preview on your GPU, and publishes only when you approve.

Publishing is your call: the checks only say the clip rendered and still looks like you, not that it looks good.
